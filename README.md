# Blue Stinger Director's Cut

A hack that ports the English translation of Blue Stinger over to the Japanese version, letting players experience the original vision for the game.

## **Overview**

Blue Stinger went through a substantial camera rework prior to its overseas release, purportedly because the overseas publisher, Activision, thought that the fixed camera angles of the Japanese original (ala Resident Evil) were not exciting enough for a then next-gen game. As a result, the overseas release had an auto-centering third-person camera, which perhaps ironically, now feels more outdated than the original fixed camera angles.

YouTuber **HyperBitHero** detailed this better than I possibly could in their video <a href="https://youtu.be/uO-1ChKtBkc">The Lost Cinematography of Blue Stinger</a>. It was the inspiration for me attempting this patch, so I highly encourage you to give it a view.

The most realistic path forward for this patch was porting the English translation to the JP version as the code changes are much less invasive than porting the JP camera system to the overseas version. (Not to mention that being way beyond my skill level.)

In keeping with my vision of making a game feel 100% localized, I've made the following changes: 

- Menu tooltips now match the English version
- Item descriptions ported from the English version 
- Hardcoded JP subs suppressed during in-engine cutscenes
- New "Director's Cut" title screen texture
- Minor texture editing to make Japanese textures match USA translation (for example, "Labo Town" texture becomes "Lab Town")
- Cutscenes now match their English variants with no hardcoded JP subs
- Opening movie recut to feature "SEGA Presents" logo, new Director's Cut logo, as well as the ending recut to remove Activision logo (aka de-Activisioning)
- Minor improvements to the English USA translation, like removing minor legal "CYA" edits. (For example, the in-game hotel name was changed from "Hillton" to "Maple" in the USA version, presumably to avoid any association with Hilton Hotels. The JP original is now restored.)

And new enhancements:
- Infinite Health and Infinite Ammo options (aka "Dad Mode")
- **Why choose?** Pick from JP or USA camera angles on the fly!
- New **FREE** camera! For the first time ever, true dual analog camera movement in Blue Stinger (if using an emulator or controller that supports a second Dreamcast analog stick)

## **Patching Instructions**

<img align="right" width="275" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8b9ebec7-4b92-45fb-bf2f-01034a56dacc">

1. Download <a href="https://github.com/DerekPascarella/UniversalDreamcastPatcher">Universal Dreamcast Patcher</a> (if you don't already have it) 
1. Select the **'Apply Patch'** tab
1. Click **'Source disc image'** to open the source disc image.
1. Click **'DCP patch file'** to locate and open the <kbd>Blue_Stinger_Directors_Cut_Exxistance_v1.0.dcp</kbd> DCP patch file.
1. Click **'Patched disc image output folder'** to select where you want to save the patched GDI
1. Click **'Apply Patch'** to generate the patched GDI, which will be saved in the folder from which the application is launched.
1. Once patching it complete, you can close the application. (Or just sit and admire how beautiful the v2.0 version looks.)
